Practice checklist

Learn by doing

Work in a personal developer instance where available. Use sample data and never enter real confidential information.

Suggested approach: Learn a concept, try it yourself, explain what happened, and revisit the relevant official documentation.

Platform and data

  • Navigate an application; filter a list and personalise a form.
  • Inspect a table, its fields and relationships; create a sample record.
  • Explore import sets and transform maps with sample data.

Users and security

  • Create test users and groups; explore role assignments.
  • Learn how access controls protect records and fields; test with an appropriate non-admin account.

Self-service and workflows

  • Explore incidents, requests and knowledge articles.
  • Create a simple catalog item with variables in your practice instance.
  • Build and test a basic flow; explore notifications and approvals.

Configuration and maintenance

  • Compare UI policies and business rules; explore update sets and when to use them.
  • Review basic reporting, CMDB concepts and platform security in the official documentation.

Certification preparation

Prepare with confidence

1

Check the latest blueprint

Review the current exam objectives and requirements. Use it as your checklist rather than relying on old exam summaries.

2

Complete relevant official learning

ServiceNow lists Welcome to ServiceNow and ServiceNow Administration Fundamentals as recommended preparation. Check the latest requirements in ServiceNow University.

3

Practise and review

Revisit weak topics using official training and product documentation. Validate your understanding with hands-on practice rather than memorising answers.

4

Verify registration details

Confirm current eligibility, any required training or voucher, pricing, exam delivery and maintenance rules directly with ServiceNow before booking.
